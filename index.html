CASE 02 — NETWORK OPERATIONS

NetOps
Sentinel.

A GNS3-based network operations lab with three FRRouting routers, five switches and five endpoint LANs. Python validates structured inventory, runs read-only reachability probes and records JSON evidence. The documented baseline uses static routing; OSPF is not claimed as implemented.

BA / 01   —   PROFILE

My starting point is the network. My curiosity goes further.

Nouakchott, Mauritania · Open to remote opportunities

I’m Baba Ahmed El Bekay, a BTS Network Security student at MIFITT Institute. I’m drawn to the infrastructure beneath the interface: how devices communicate, where trust boundaries sit, how services are exposed, and what happens when something fails.

My learning path combines formal networking studies with hands-on labs and independent builds. I’ve worked with routing and switching concepts, Linux systems, cloud identity fundamentals and Python automation. Projects like NetSec Agent and NetOps Sentinel let me turn those foundations into tools and repeatable tests—not just notes.

I also enjoy the communication side of engineering: keeping a clear record, stating limitations honestly and making technical work understandable to the next person. I speak Arabic natively, with advanced English and French, and I’m currently looking for a remote internship where I can contribute while learning from an experienced team.
